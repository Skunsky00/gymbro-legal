Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Last updated November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Spotter: Gym Partner & Dating app and related services (“Spotter” or the “Service”) collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. The Service is provided by Jacob Johnson, an individual doing business as Spotter (“we,” “us,” “our”). By using the Service, you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Use.

The short version. We collect what we need to run a gym-partner and dating app: your phone number, profile details, photos, and messages. Other users see your profile, but not your phone number and not your private Dating preferences. We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t show ads. You can delete your account in the app.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: your US mobile phone number, used to sign in.
  • Profile information: your first name, birthday, gender, who you want to train with, your home gym (name, address, and map coordinates), the times you train, training styles, experience level, fitness goal, optional lift numbers, photos, and an optional caption.
  • Activity in the app: the people you like or skip, comments you add to likes, matches, chat messages, session proposals, and reports or blocks you submit.
  • Dating preferences (if you turn on Dating mode): who you are interested in, your preferred age range, and distance. These are private. See Section 3.
  • Communications with us: messages you send to our support email.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical information: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and logs of requests to our servers.
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, an identifier that lets us send them to your device.
  • Usage information: for example, how many likes you have sent today, which we use to apply daily limits.
  • Security signals: information our providers use to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app on a genuine device.
  • Diagnostics: we may use crash-reporting and analytics tools (such as Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase) to find and fix problems and understand how the app is used.

Location

We use the location of the gym you choose to show you people nearby. If you allow location access, the app may use your device’s location while you search for a gym, to suggest gyms near you. We do not keep a running record of your device’s location. You can turn location access off in your iPhone’s Settings.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Spotter+, Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your card number or Apple ID details. We receive information about your subscription status (such as whether it is active and when it expires) from Apple and from RevenueCat, a service we use to manage subscriptions.

2. How we use information

  • To create and run your account, verify your phone number, and keep you signed in.
  • To show your profile to other users, suggest people to you, record likes and matches, and let matches chat.
  • To apply daily limits and provide Spotter+ features.
  • To keep the Service safe: checking photos, captions, comments, and messages against our rules; reviewing reports; hiding or removing accounts; preventing fraud, spam, and abuse; and enforcing our Terms.
  • To send notifications about matches, messages, and session proposals, and service messages such as verification codes.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To fix bugs, maintain security, and improve the Service.
  • To comply with the law and respond to valid legal requests.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own advertising, and we do not show ads in the app.

3. What other people can see

Your profile is visible to other users. Other Spotter users can see your first name, age (not your birthday), photos, caption, gym name and approximate distance, training schedule, training styles, experience, goal, and any lift numbers you add. Do not put anything in your profile that you do not want other users to see. Content you send in a chat is visible to the person you are chatting with.

What stays private. Your phone number is not shown to other users. Your Dating preferences are private: we use them on our servers to work out whether two people are compatible, and we do not show them to other users. Likes you have received are shown to you, and, if you do not have Spotter+, only as a count.

Sensitive information. Your gender and Dating preferences may reveal information about your sexual orientation, and your photos may reveal other sensitive characteristics. We use this information only to provide the Service as described here, and not to infer characteristics about you or for advertising.

4. Photo, text, and chat checks

  • Photos. When you upload a photo, it is first held privately and checked by an automated image-analysis service (Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch). Photos that pass are made visible to other users. Photos that fail are deleted, and we keep a record of the result and a count of rejections for safety purposes.
  • Captions, comments, and messages. These may be checked by automated filters. Content that fails may be blocked or removed, and we may keep a private copy and a record against the account.
  • Reports. If someone reports you or your content, or something is flagged, we may review your profile, photos, and related conversations to decide what to do. We may hide or remove accounts, and block a phone number from signing up again.

5. How we share information

We share information in these situations:

  • With other users, as described in Section 3.
  • With service providers who process information for us under their own terms and privacy policies:
    • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): authentication and phone verification, database, file storage, server functions, push notifications, image analysis, and diagnostics;
    • Apple: the App Store, subscription payments, push notifications, and map search for gyms;
    • RevenueCat: subscription management (it receives an account identifier and purchase and subscription information);
    • An email delivery service (Resend): to send us internal safety alerts, which may include account identifiers and report details;
    • Mobile carriers, who deliver verification text messages.
  • For safety and legal reasons: to law enforcement or other authorities where we believe the law requires it, to respond to valid legal process, to report suspected child sexual exploitation as required by law, to protect the rights, safety, or property of you, other users, or us, and to enforce our Terms.
  • In a business transaction, such as a merger, sale, or if we move the Service to a company we form. We will tell you if your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

We may also use or share information that has been aggregated or de-identified so that it no longer identifies you.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your profile, photos, likes, preferences, and related account data, and we end your matches. Some things may remain:

  • Messages you sent may remain visible to the people you chatted with.
  • Reports, moderation records, and records that an account was removed may be kept for as long as we need them for safety, fraud prevention, and legal reasons. This includes information we use to stop a removed person from signing up again.
  • Copies may remain in backups and logs for a limited time.
  • Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their policies.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. You can manage subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

7. Your choices

  • Edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app, under Profile.
  • Notifications can be turned off in your iPhone’s Settings.
  • Location access can be turned off in your iPhone’s Settings.
  • Dating mode can be turned off in the app, and your Dating preferences remain private either way.
  • Contact us at travelbroche@gmail.com to ask about, correct, or delete your information.

Choose a phone passcode, keep your device up to date, and tell us right away if you think your account has been accessed by someone else.
