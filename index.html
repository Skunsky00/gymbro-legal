Support

Questions, problems, or something you want to report? Email travelbroche@gmail.com. You can also report or block a person from inside the app.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

Common questions

  • Delete my account: in the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account. This does not cancel a subscription.
  • Cancel Spotter+: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Refunds: subscriptions are billed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
